## SynthXRay3D

Master's dissertation, in progress, on generative models for medical imaging.

Orthopedic surgery planning relies mostly on 2D radiographs, while the volumetric data that complex procedures need comes from CT scans, which are expensive, less accessible and deliver a higher radiation dose. The work adapts generative architectures to synthesise several radiographic views from a single X-ray image, so that 3D reconstruction of bone structures can be driven by ordinary radiographs, and measures how much that synthesis improves the reconstruction downstream.
